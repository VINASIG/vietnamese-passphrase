# Research that agents can and cannot establish

The project requires SI-agent execution and assessment. This roadmap does not recruit people or make a human study a completion gate. It separates useful additional automated work from claims that require observations of Vietnamese people. Missing observations remain missing.

## Additional automated research

| Uncertainty                   | Agent-executable work and inspectable output                                                                                                   | What a positive result could establish                                   | What it cannot establish                                          |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Corpus and domain bias        | Add independently licensed sources from different domains, record provenance and overlap, report per-source coverage and threshold sensitivity | Coverage robustness across those exact sources and domains               | Population familiarity or regional representativeness             |
| Ambiguous meaning and content | Inspect licensed definitions and usage contexts, record sense-level decisions, disagreements and unresolved items                              | Broader documented semantic screening under a specified policy           | What particular users find offensive or appropriate               |
| Boundary and Unicode behavior | Test NFC/NFD, grapheme counts, bytes, allowed separators, fonts, copy/paste and published input-method implementations                         | Exact format and rendering behavior in tested implementations            | Readability, remembered boundaries or human confusion rates       |
| Confusability models          | Expand published linguistic scenarios and keystroke models with matched controls, full pairs and independent oracles                           | Properties of each explicit model and sensitivity to assumptions         | Actual homophone coverage or regional error probabilities         |
| Profile optimization          | Compare several targets and profiles with declared objectives, evaluate graph-selection alternatives and preserve held-out sources             | Engineering dominance and constraint satisfaction under those objectives | A human-optimal vocabulary or universally best objective weights  |
| Generator and delivery trust  | Independent implementation vectors, fuzzed parsers, adversarial manifests, reproducible environments and verified provenance                   | Covered implementation invariants and artifact origin                    | Trustworthy maintainers, independent review or production fitness |

Each investigation must state its hypothesis, source and license, controlled variables, output files and failure criteria before interpreting results. A corpus coverage increase remains a corpus coverage increase. Model agreement is not participant agreement. A simulated typing distribution is labeled a simulation, never a measured user-error rate. Multiple automated proxies cannot be averaged into a human memorability score without validation.

## Claims that require Vietnamese participants

| Claim                               | Necessary direct observations                                     | Important controls                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Familiarity and regional acceptance | Real speakers' recognition and meaning judgements                 | Age, region, education, vocabulary domain and sense                                   |
| Better immediate or delayed recall  | Recall of assigned disposable phrases after actual delays         | Matched selection space, format, length, learning exposure and comparison vocabulary  |
| Lower entry error or effort         | Actual entries, errors, corrections and elapsed time              | Device, keyboard, Telex/VNI/mobile input, accent mode and task context                |
| Appropriate general-purpose content | Contextual assessments by relevant users and institutions         | Intended audience, uncommon senses and generated combinations                         |
| Safe behavior after generation      | Observed rewriting, reordering, substitutions and reuse decisions | Realistic instructions and downstream constraints, without collecting account secrets |

An EFF-translated, manually curated or historical Vietnamese baseline can be a comparator, not assumed ground truth. A future authorized study would need a registered protocol, suitable sample size based on power and the effect of interest, consent, data minimization, counterbalancing and honest uncertainty estimates. A convenient sample of one hundred speakers is not automatically representative. Published Vietnamese linguistic or user studies can inform hypotheses, but do not validate these exact vocabularies, profiles or representations unless they actually tested them.

Until those observations exist, this project may claim transparent agent assessment, structural measurements, tested invariants and reproducible artifacts. It may not claim easier human recall, fewer human errors, regional approval, superiority over a human-curated baseline, or a Vietnamese passphrase standard. Continuing automated work is useful under that boundary.
